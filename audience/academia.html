Try to break it

DD&SA is not asking for your endorsement. It is asking for your best attack.

The full corpus — more than sixty published instruments, a complete written constitution, and the supporting methodology — is free to read in the Reading Room. Every mechanism is specified: selection, stratification, deliberation, decision, justice, fiscal constraint, crisis override, and removal. Nothing is hidden behind a paywall, a membership, or a footnote that says “details to follow.”

The design has been red-teamed — but not by you

During development, the architecture was tested against a deliberately constructed set of hostile institutional objections — the strongest cases that constitutional lawyers, party strategists, civil servants, and editors would be expected to make against it, each argued in its own register. That was an internal adversarial design exercise, not external peer review, and it is presented here as method rather than credential. Its strongest objections forced structural revisions, including the Checked Assembly Model and four native removability mechanisms, culminating in the Civic Recall & Reversibility Act. The system now carries its own off-switch: residents can reverse any decision, or remove the architecture entirely, using its own rules.

What the design has not yet survived is you. No independent academic critique has been published against the corpus. That gap is not a boast — it is the standing invitation.

The challenge

Debunk it. Find the incentive gradient the internal exercise missed, the capture vector, the failure cascade, the constitutional contradiction. Publish it, teach it, or send it directly — serious critique receives a serious reply from the architect, and both are logged permanently on the Scrutiny Register.

But the challenge has a price of victory. If you break DD&SA, the work does not end with the demolition. You must then produce something better — better than the party-political system whose failures are documented and cited throughout this site, and better than DD&SA itself. Critique that ends at “no” leaves the betrayals of the current settlement exactly where they are.

Where to start

The Full Corpus for the instruments themselves; the Blueprint for the architecture in overview; the Stratification page for the selection mathematics; and the contact form for correspondence — include a document reference and it will be answered on the substance.
