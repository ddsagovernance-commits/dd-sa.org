Get Involved

Why Get Involved?

DD&SA is a democratic architecture built on resident participation. It works only when people engage — not as spectators, but as contributors. Getting involved does not require expertise, political alignment, or prior experience. It requires only a willingness to think, question, and participate in good faith.

1. Learn the Basics

Understanding the structure is the first step. Explore the core pages:

  • What is DD&SA?
  • DD&SA Blueprint
  • Essays
  • Video Library

These pages explain the architecture, the reasoning behind it, and the structural problems it aims to solve.

2. Share the Idea

DD&SA grows through conversation. You can:

  • share the explainer video
  • send the plain-English explainer
  • discuss the model with friends, colleagues, or community groups
  • link to the site on social platforms

The goal is not persuasion. The goal is clarity — making the architecture visible so people can evaluate it.

3. Participate in Local Discussions

DD&SA is designed to operate at multiple scales, including local communities. You can:

  • host small discussion groups
  • organise reading sessions around the Blueprint
  • invite others to explore the model

These discussions help identify questions, concerns, and opportunities for refinement.

4. Contribute Skills

DD&SA is a structural project. It benefits from contributions in:

  • web development
  • design and visual communication
  • research and analysis
  • community organisation
  • writing and editing

If you have skills you want to contribute, you can help strengthen the clarity and accessibility of the model.

5. Stay Informed

The fastest way to stay aligned with the project is the mailing list — one email a month as DD&SA moves from blueprint to pilot, starting with the first assembly in Surrey Heath. No spam, no fundraising, unsubscribe anytime.

As the project develops, new materials will be added here as well:

  • downloadable resources
  • diagrams and visual explanations
  • additional videos
  • case studies and examples

6. Why This Matters

DD&SA is not about ideology. It is about structure — how decisions are made, how legitimacy is built, and how residents participate in shaping their collective future. Getting involved means contributing to a democratic architecture designed for transparency, honesty, and long‑term thinking.
