Cite this work

The DD&SA corpus is free to read, download, and cite. Suggested formats are below; adapt them to whichever style guide you're working in.

A single document

Graham, Ian R. (2026). The Sortition Architecture (DDSA-SORT-001). DD&SA — Direct Democracy & Sortition Assemblies. https://dd-sa.org/corpus/

Replace the title, reference code, and link with the document you're citing. Every corpus document carries a reference code on its cover.

The corpus as a whole

Graham, Ian R. (2026). Direct Democracy & Sortition Assemblies: A Civic Architecture for the British Isles. https://dd-sa.org

In-text / short reference

(Graham, 2026, DD&SA)

For a formal citation of a specific edition, get in touch via the contact page.
