Transparency & How We Work

Transparency is one of DD&SA's founding principles — and it applies to this website as much as it applies to the assemblies the project describes. This page explains what DD&SA is, how the content on this site is created, and the basis on which it is offered.

Transparency Note

Direct Democracy & Sortition Assemblies is a resident‑led initiative. The ideas and explanations on this site will evolve as the project develops. Everything here is written to help residents understand how DD&SA works and how it could serve our communities.

If you spot anything unclear or think something could be improved, your feedback is welcome — this project grows stronger through resident participation. You can reach us through the contact page.

How We Create Content

Some text on this website is drafted with digital tools and then reviewed and edited by the architect involved in designing DD&SA. Final explanations, positions, and decisions always come from a human — never automated systems.

This approach helps us produce clear, accessible information while keeping human judgement at the centre of the project.

Legal Note

This website provides general information about Direct Democracy & Sortition Assemblies. It is not legal advice. All content is subject to revision as the project develops. DD&SA is designed to be a community‑driven initiative and does not claim to represent official government policy.
